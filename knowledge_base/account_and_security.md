# Lumen Home — Account & Security

## Account basics

One Lumen account per email address. Household members can be invited under a Lumen+ Family plan (Account → Household → Invite), each getting their own login with configurable permissions (Admin, Standard, Child).

**Changing your email:** Account → Profile → Email. A verification link is sent to the new address; the change doesn't take effect until confirmed, and your old email remains active until then.

**Changing your password:** Account → Security → Change Password. Requires entering your current password. If you don't remember it, use "Forgot Password" from the login screen instead.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA is optional but strongly recommended, especially for accounts with cameras. Enable it under Account → Security → Two-Factor Authentication. Supported methods: SMS code or an authenticator app (TOTP, e.g. Google Authenticator, Authy). We recommend the authenticator app method — SMS-based 2FA is more vulnerable to SIM-swap attacks.

**Lost 2FA access:** If you lose access to your 2FA method (lost phone, uninstalled authenticator), account recovery requires identity verification through support, including confirming account details and a valid order number. This process can take up to 3 business days for security reasons — there is no instant bypass, including for support agents.

## Data & privacy

Camera recordings are encrypted in transit and at rest. Recordings are stored in the region matching your account's registered country (US or EU) and are not moved across regions.

**Deleting your account:** Account → Security → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all recordings, device pairings, and account data after a 30-day grace period (during which you can cancel the deletion by logging back in). Active subscriptions are canceled immediately upon requesting deletion, not at the end of the grace period.

**Data export:** You can request a copy of your account data (device list, event history metadata — not video files) under Account → Privacy → Request My Data. This is generated within 48 hours and emailed as a download link valid for 7 days.

## Suspicious activity

If a customer reports unrecognized devices or logins on their account: direct them to Account → Security → Active Sessions, where they can revoke any session remotely. Recommend they also change their password and enable 2FA if not already active. If they report their camera feed was accessed without authorization, this should be escalated to a human agent immediately — do not attempt to resolve a suspected security breach through automated troubleshooting alone.
