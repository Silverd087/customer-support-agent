# Lumen Home — Shipping & Returns Policy

## Shipping

**Processing time:** Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the same business day. Orders placed after 2pm ET or on weekends/holidays ship the next business day.

**Shipping options and estimated transit time (US only):**
- Standard: 4–6 business days, free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99
- Expedited: 2–3 business days, $12.99
- Overnight: next business day if ordered before noon ET, $24.99

International shipping is currently available to Canada and the UK only, 7–14 business days, calculated at checkout.

**Order tracking:** A tracking link is emailed once your order ships. Tracking can also be viewed by signing into your account and opening Order History.

**Lost or damaged in transit:** Contact support within 14 days of the expected delivery date for lost packages, or within 7 days of delivery for damaged items, and we'll replace the item at no cost or issue a full refund.

## Returns

**Return window:** 30 days from the delivery date for a full refund, provided the item is in its original packaging with all accessories. Devices that have been physically damaged by the customer (cracked casing, water damage outside rated specs, etc.) are not eligible for a full refund but may qualify for a partial credit at our discretion.

**How to start a return:** Sign in, go to Order History, select the item, and choose "Start a Return." You'll receive a prepaid return shipping label by email. Drop the package at any carrier location within 14 days of generating the label.

**Refund timing:** Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the returned item.

**Exchanges:** We don't process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement — this is faster than waiting for a return to be received before shipping a replacement.

**Non-returnable items:** Gift cards, and any item marked "Final Sale" at checkout, are not eligible for return.

## Warranty (separate from returns)

All Lumen devices include a 1-year limited hardware warranty covering manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date. Warranty claims after the 30-day return window go through a repair-or-replace process rather than a refund — see the Warranty section of the Troubleshooting Guide for details. Extended 2-year warranty coverage is included automatically with an active Lumen+ subscription.
