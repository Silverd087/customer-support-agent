# Lumen Home — Billing & Subscriptions (Lumen+)

## Plans

- **Free:** device control, live camera view, motion push notifications, up to 50 devices.
- **Lumen+ Individual:** $6.99/month or $59.99/year — adds 30-day cloud video history, unlimited devices, 2-year extended warranty, priority support (4-hour response target).
- **Lumen+ Family:** $11.99/month or $99.99/year — everything in Individual, shared across up to 6 household members, each with their own login.

Prices shown are in USD; local pricing/taxes apply outside the US.

## Billing cycle and charges

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same day of the month (or year) as the original purchase. If a renewal date falls on a day that doesn't exist in a given month (e.g., the 31st), it renews on the last day of that month instead.

A failed renewal payment triggers up to 3 retry attempts over 7 days. If all retries fail, the account is downgraded to Free and cloud recordings begin the 14-day deletion countdown described in the FAQ.

## Changing or canceling a plan

**Upgrading** (Free → Lumen+, or Individual → Family) takes effect immediately; you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period.

**Downgrading or canceling** takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep Lumen+ features until then, and are not charged again after that. There is no partial refund for the unused portion of a billing period when you cancel.

**Switching monthly to annual billing** (or vice versa) can be done anytime in Account → Subscription. The new cycle starts at the next renewal date.

## Refund policy for subscriptions

Subscription charges are refundable within 14 days of the charge date if you haven't used more than a trivial amount of Lumen+-only features (e.g., haven't viewed cloud recordings). Requests after 14 days, or after meaningful usage, are handled case-by-case by support — we do not guarantee a refund past that window.

## Payment methods

We accept major credit/debit cards and PayPal. We do not currently support ACH bank transfer, cryptocurrency, or invoicing for individual consumer accounts. Business/enterprise accounts (10+ locations) should contact sales for invoicing options — this is outside the scope of standard consumer support.

## Common billing questions

**Why was I charged twice this month?** Almost always one of: (a) you upgraded mid-cycle, so you see the prorated upgrade charge plus a separate line for the original period, or (b) you have two Lumen accounts with active subscriptions under the same payment method. Check Account → Subscription on each account.

**Can I get an invoice/receipt?** Yes — Account → Billing History → select the charge → "Download Receipt." Receipts are PDF and include tax breakdown where applicable.
