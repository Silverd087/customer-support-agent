# Lumen Home — Frequently Asked Questions

Lumen Home makes smart home devices (cameras, plugs, thermostats, sensors) and the Lumen+ companion app/subscription.

## Getting started

**How do I set up a new Lumen device?**
Download the Lumen Home app (iOS or Android), create an account, and tap "Add Device." Hold the power button on your device for 5 seconds until the LED blinks blue, then follow the in-app pairing steps. Most devices pair over Wi-Fi 2.4GHz — Lumen devices do not support 5GHz networks.

**Which Wi-Fi networks are supported?**
2.4GHz networks only, WPA2 or WPA3 security. If your router broadcasts a combined 2.4/5GHz network under one name, you may need to temporarily split the bands during setup, then merge them back afterward.

**Can I control Lumen devices without the app?**
Yes, if you've linked a Lumen account to Amazon Alexa or Google Home, you can use voice commands. Direct Wi-Fi control without any app or account is not supported for security reasons.

## Devices

**How many devices can I connect to one account?**
Up to 50 devices on the Free plan, unlimited on Lumen+.

**Do Lumen cameras work without a subscription?**
Live view and motion push notifications work on the Free plan. Cloud video recording (7-day or 30-day rolling history) requires a Lumen+ subscription.

**What happens to my recordings if I cancel Lumen+?**
Cloud recordings are retained for 14 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted. Download anything you want to keep before that window closes.

## Troubleshooting basics

**My device shows offline in the app.**
Check that the device's LED is solid (not blinking red), confirm your Wi-Fi password hasn't changed, and try power-cycling the device (unplug for 10 seconds). If it's still offline after that, see the full Troubleshooting Guide document for device-specific steps.

**I forgot my account password.**
Use "Forgot Password" on the login screen. A reset link is sent to your account email and expires after 30 minutes.

## Contacting support

Support is available via in-app chat, email (support@lumenhome.example), and phone for Lumen+ subscribers. Response time targets: chat and email within 1 business day on Free, within 4 hours on Lumen+.
