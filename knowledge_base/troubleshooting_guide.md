# Lumen Home — Troubleshooting Guide

## Device shows "Offline"

1. Check the LED: solid blue = online, blinking red = Wi-Fi connection lost, off entirely = power issue.
2. Confirm the Wi-Fi network is 2.4GHz — Lumen devices cannot join 5GHz-only networks.
3. Power-cycle the device: unplug or hold the reset button for 10 seconds, wait 30 seconds, reconnect power.
4. If your router password changed recently, remove the device in the app and re-pair it — devices don't auto-update stored Wi-Fi credentials.
5. Still offline after the above: move the device closer to the router temporarily to rule out range issues, and check the app's "Network Diagnostics" tool under Device Settings.

If none of this resolves it within 24 hours of the device going offline, escalate to a support ticket — this may indicate a hardware fault, which is covered by warranty.

## Camera-specific issues

**No live view / spinning loader:** Usually a network bandwidth issue. Confirm no other device is saturating the connection (large downloads, video calls). Lumen cameras need roughly 1–2 Mbps upload per stream.

**Motion notifications not arriving:** Check Settings → Notifications is enabled both in-app and at the OS level (iOS/Android notification permissions). Also check the motion sensitivity setting hasn't been set to "Low" or "Off" under Device Settings → Motion.

**Night vision not working / image too dark:** Confirm the device isn't in "Color Night Vision" mode in a completely dark room (it needs a small amount of ambient or IR light source unless in standard IR night vision mode). Try toggling Device Settings → Night Vision → Auto.

## Thermostat-specific issues

**Temperature reading seems wrong:** Allow 15 minutes after installation for the sensor to calibrate to the room. Avoid installing near direct sunlight, vents, or exterior doors, which will cause inaccurate readings by design, not a defect.

**Schedule not running:** Confirm "Smart Schedule" (auto-adjusting based on presence detection) isn't overriding your manual schedule — check Device Settings → Schedule Mode.

## Warranty claims (after the 30-day return window)

If a device has a hardware fault covered by the 1-year (or 2-year, for Lumen+) warranty and troubleshooting above hasn't resolved it:

1. Open a support ticket with your order number and a description of the fault.
2. Support will confirm the issue isn't a setup/configuration problem via the diagnostics above.
3. Approved claims result in a replacement device shipped at no cost, with a prepaid label to return the faulty unit within 14 days of receiving the replacement.
4. Devices not returned within that window will be charged to the original payment method at full price.

Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage beyond the device's IP rating, or unauthorized modification.

## Escalation

If a customer has been through the relevant steps above and the issue persists, or reports something safety-related (smoke, unusual heat, sparking), escalate to a human agent immediately rather than continuing troubleshooting steps.
